Permit & outfitter details

Entry Point 30 (Lake One). Permit #BW-2025-4471. Outfitter: Ely Canoe. Shuttle departs 7:30 AM from Moose Lake Rd.

Campsite research — Knife Lake

Site #3 on northeast shore has the best wind protection. Two decent flat tent pads. Thor's Oak visible from shore — worth the 0.5 mi paddle north.

Campsites Updated May 29

Emergency contacts

Cook County Sheriff: 218-387-3030. USFS BWCA: 218-626-4300. Nearest hospital: Ely hospital, 85 mi south.
